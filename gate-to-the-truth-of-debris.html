Curiosity

Gale Crater · driving since Aug 2012

Still climbing Mount Sharp, still sniffing for organic chemistry. Over 33 km driven and counting. Its wheels are slowly shredding — every dent is logged from orbit.

● ACTIVE

Perseverance

Jezero Crater · driving since Feb 2021

Caching rock samples for a future trip home, and occasionally pausing to watch Ingenuity's replacement dust. It even kept a "safety zone" around its fallen helicopter friend.

Chapter V · The Long Silence

Time since their last word

Live, and still counting — measured to the second, from this page to the day each machine went quiet.

"My battery is low and it's getting dark."
— Opportunity's final transmission, June 10 2018 (as the NASA scientist describing it put it — a paraphrase that became its epitaph)

None of these machines was truly lost. They were spent — deliberately, gloriously — in exchange for knowledge no human eye could gather. The Moon buggies still sit where astronauts parked them, with tire tracks that will outlast every city on Earth. Sojourner's solar panels are gathering dust a few meters from Pathfinder. Spirit sleeps in the sand she was stuck in. Opportunity rests at the rim of Endeavour.

Abandoned, but not forgotten. Drive on.
